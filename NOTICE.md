# Technical references

The OpenCode Zen integration follows the authenticated billing flow implemented by OpenCode and independently documented by CodexBar:

- OpenCode console billing source: <https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode>
- CodexBar OpenCode Zen balance integration: <https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar/commit/d24664bb4e15a575add50bfebcdf4040310227dd>
